Executive Group Assignment Summary

Comparative Evaluation of Electronic Component & Technology-Driven Industrial Sectors in Malaysia

This FIN 430 group assignment evaluates the financial performance, stability, and operational efficiency of two prominent Malaysian companies—Amlex Holdings Berhad and Turiya Berhad—across financial years 2021 and 2022[cite: 23, 24, 25]. The study uses comprehensive ratio analyses spanning liquidity, activity (efficiency), leverage (solvency), profitability, and market valuation to contrast how each firm manages short-term obligations, capital structure, and shareholder wealth[cite: 23].

Company 1

Amlex Holdings Berhad

A leading Malaysian manufacturer and supplier of high-quality electronic components, connectors, terminals, and customized solutions serving automotive and consumer electronics industries globally[cite: 24]. Demonstrated remarkable growth in EPS (from 1.26 Sen to 4.60 Sen) and strong operational efficiency improvements in 2022[cite: 32, 38].

Company 2

Turiya Berhad

A technology-driven solutions provider focusing on commercial, natural, healthcare technologies, and sustainability/environmental innovations[cite: 25]. Maintained robust liquidity ratios but faced operational activity headwinds such as extended average collection periods and declining gross profit margins[cite: 29, 31, 36].
