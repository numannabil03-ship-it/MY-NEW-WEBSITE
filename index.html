Executive Summary & Placement Scope

Transforming Academic Theory into Corporate Credit Rigor at Menara CelcomDigi

Spanning a rigorous 24-week tenure from March to August 2026 at CTOS Basis Sdn Bhd, stationed within Menara CelcomDigi, Petaling Jaya, my role as an Operations Finance Intern served as the definitive bridge between my academic foundations in BBA Investment Management from Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) Segamat and high-stakes corporate credit assessment. Operating at the forefront of Malaysia’s credit reporting agency ecosystem, my core responsibilities revolved around rigorous financial statement evaluation, corporate background verification, and risk auditing across thousands of commercial entities.

Core Operational Responsibilities

Auditing 20–30 Corporate Financial Statements Daily

In an industry where data fidelity dictates institutional lending decisions, precision is non-negotiable. On a daily basis, I systematically audited, vetted, and processed between 20 to 30 complex corporate financial statements—scrutinizing Profit & Loss statements, balance sheets, and cash flow liquidity ratios.

My daily workflow involved cross-verifying raw financial figures against official Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) filings and RAM Credit Information databases. This ensured that commercial credit scores, director directorship linkages, and litigation history tracking reports generated for banking institutions and enterprise clients maintained 100% accuracy. Under high-pressure turnaround windows (such as bulk-auditing 50 corporate files within tight 2-hour SLAs), I leveraged advanced spreadsheet modeling (VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, and pivot structures) to flag liquidity stress and anomalous liability spikes before credit committee approvals.

Strategic Impact & Risk Management

Mitigating Corporate Default Risk in Malaysia's Economic Landscape

Working within CTOS Basis provided deep insight into macro-level corporate default indicators. By analyzing debt-to-equity structures, working capital deficits, and director background histories, I developed an acute understanding of how macroeconomic headwinds translate into corporate credit distress.

The internship significantly sharpened my ability to conduct comprehensive SWOT analyses of distressed corporate balance sheets, ensuring that financial institutions received transparent, unvarnished risk disclosures. This experience cemented my expertise in credit risk mitigation, regulatory compliance under Malaysian credit laws, and enterprise operational efficiency.

Career Readiness & Future Outlook

The 24-week stint at CTOS Basis successfully equipped me with institutional-grade financial analysis acumen, reinforcing my readiness to excel in corporate banking, fixed income analysis, and investment portfolio management.

Strategic Synthesis, Leverage Dynamics, and Market Psychology

Strategic Direction & "Weight of Evidence"

Phase 1 synthesis focused on macro indicators—such as GDP and OPR trajectories—combined with technical indicators like moving averages, RSI, and Bollinger Bands. This "Weight of Evidence" methodology ensures foundational catalysts provide the rationale ("why"), while technical signals dictate timing ("when")[cite: 17].

Leverage & Risk Responsibility

Holding 10 contracts of FKLI with a RM40,000 initial margin requirement highlighted how index point multipliers magnify both gains and losses. Leverage is a double-edged sword that requires rigorous liquidity management and strict observance of maintenance margins to prevent catastrophic equity drawdowns.

Decision-Making Under Market Pressure

Daily price fluctuations challenged adherence to our directional thesis. Observing Bollinger Band boundaries and volume confirmation proved essential in evaluating whether market trends were exhausting or accelerating, bridging classroom theory with the realities of professional derivatives trading[cite: 17, 18].
