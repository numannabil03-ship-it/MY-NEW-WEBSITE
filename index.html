INS 510: Introduction to Takaful

Microtakaful in Malaysia: Financial Safety Net for B40

Prepared by: Nu'man Nabil, Iskandar Zunnurain, Danish Imran, Amir Syahrin | Lecturer: Dr. Nur Hanisah Razali

Core Research Findings

This study investigates whether Microtakaful can function as a viable investment vehicle or if its primary role must remain focused as an accessible risk-protection mechanism for low-income (B40) groups in Malaysia.

  • Protection vs Investment Dual Purpose: Microtakaful premiums remain very small (a few Ringgits per month). Attempting to blend investment features raises premium costs, conflicting with the fundamental mission of cheap social safety net protection.
  • Shariah Compliance & Risk Pooling: Operating under mutual assistance (Tabarru'), Microtakaful prevents B40 households from falling into severe poverty due to unforeseen medical emergencies, disability, or death.
  • Distribution Strategy: Recommends partnering with digital fintech platforms and microfinance providers to expand rural penetration across East & West Malaysia.
